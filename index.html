Kingdom of Heaven – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Kingdom of Heaven (package name com.maximozzilla.koh) handles your information. The short version: the game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. Kingdom of Heaven has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no in-app purchases. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send anything about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

The game keeps the following only on your own device, so it can work:

  • Your game progress (your resources, buildings, villagers, soldiers, level and achievements).
  • The name you give your King, and your sound and message settings.

We never see this information. You can erase your progress at any time using Settings → Factory Reset in the game, or by uninstalling the app.

Android backup

If Android's own backup feature is turned on for your Google account, Android may include your saved game progress in that backup, so your kingdom can be restored if you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. This backup is created and stored by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; we cannot access it. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

Contacting support

If you choose to email us, we will receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you and will not share it with anyone.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. In any case, because it collects no personal data, no personal data is collected from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example if a future version adds new features, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
